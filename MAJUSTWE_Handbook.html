1. Name & Objectives

Q: What is the official name of the welfare?
A: The official name is the Matuga Junior Schools Teachers' Welfare (MAJUSTWE).
Q: What are the core objectives of MAJUSTWE?
A:
  • To unite all Matuga junior school teachers through interactive engagements, open forums, and meaningful activities.
  • To offer moral, social, and financial support to teachers during times of bereavement and other testing times.
  • To disseminate information to members on matters pertaining to education.
  • To pool resources and invest in various projects for the welfare of the members.

2. Membership & Joining

Q: Who can join MAJUSTWE?
A: Membership is open to all willing teachers teaching in junior schools (under PnP, Interns, and B.O.M terms) within the Matuga sub-county.
Q: What is the difference between Ordinary and Associate members?
A: Ordinary Members are those currently teaching in Matuga sub-county. Associate Members are teachers who joined while serving in Matuga but have since transferred out or retired.
Q: What details do I need to provide during registration, and what are they used for?
A: During your online registration, you are strictly required to declare the following information:
  • Next of Kin & Beneficiaries: To ensure that in the unfortunate event of your demise, the welfare benefits are disbursed to the exact individual you authorized, preventing family disputes.
  • Dependents (Max 4 Children): So the welfare knows exactly which children are officially covered in case of bereavement.
  • Guardians / Parents (Max 2): To register your official parents or guardians who are covered under the Kshs. 200 contribution tier.
  • Spouse (Max 1): To officially register the spouse covered under the Kshs. 500 contribution tier.
Note: This information is kept strictly confidential and is used solely by the Executive Committee to verify claims and prevent fraud. Once submitted, Guardians and Dependents can only be updated by the Secretary upon formal request.
Q: What are the financial requirements to join?
A: Upon joining, you must pay a non-refundable registration fee of Kshs. 100 and deposit a refundable fee of Kshs. 500 into the emergency kitty.
Q: How long do I have to wait before I can receive welfare benefits?
A: You must be a registered, contributing member for at least three (3) months before you are eligible to receive benefits.
Q: Can I lose my membership?
A: Yes. You can lose your membership if you fail to make contributions (depleting your emergency kitty), intentionally defraud the welfare, provide false information, or voluntarily withdraw in writing.

Q: When an emergency happens, how much do I need to contribute?
A: Contributions vary strictly based on the emergency type as outlined in Article 3:

Q: How long do I have to make a contribution?
A: You have exactly seven (7) days from the time the welfare leadership officially announces the case to the members.
Q: How fast does the affected member receive the money?
A: The welfare guarantees that bereavement contributions will reach the affected member within a maximum of seven (7) days from the confirmation of the case. A maximum of 10% of the total collected may be used for transport/logistics for the visiting delegation.

4. Welfare Leadership & Elections

Q: Who runs the welfare?
A: A 9-member Executive Committee consisting of the Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary, Vice Secretary, Treasurer, Organizing Secretary, Vice Organizing Secretary, and two Zonal Representatives. The composition must strictly adhere to the 2/3 gender rule.
Q: How are leaders elected?
A: Leaders are elected via secret ballot during a general meeting. The candidate with the majority vote wins. Office bearers serve a term of two (2) years and are eligible for re-election.
Q: Who manages the elections?
A: An independent Election Board nominated and approved by the members. Members of the board serve for 3 years and are not allowed to run for any welfare office unless they resign from the board prior to the election.

5. Finances & Investments

Q: How are the welfare's bank accounts managed?
A: The Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer are the mandatory signatories to the welfare's bank accounts. For any financial transaction to be valid, all three signatories must append their signature. Any transaction must be authorized by the management committee and minuted.
Q: How does the emergency kitty work?
A: The emergency kitty funds are used to caution a member in case of delay in contributions. After the delayed contribution is made by the member, the funds are refunded back into their emergency kitty.
Q: Does the welfare invest its money?
A: Yes. The leadership nominates an Investment Committee (approved by members) tasked with advising, managing, and reporting on investment projects to grow the welfare's resources.

6. Meetings & Activities

Q: When do we hold our meetings?
A:
  • Annual General Meeting (AGM): Held once a year during the third term of the academic year. (Requires 2 weeks' notice and a 2/3 quorum).
  • Special Meetings: Called for urgent matters. (Requires 2 weeks' notice and a 1/2 quorum).
  • Annual Get-Together: Organized once a year with a 1-month notice.

7. Dispute Resolution & Dissolution

Q: What happens if members disagree?
A: The constitution provides primary guidance. For complex disagreements, independent arbitrators may be involved to settle disputes.
Q: Can the welfare be dissolved?
A: Yes, if approved by more than 2/3 of registered members during an AGM/Special Meeting, or if ordered by a Kenyan court of law. Upon dissolution, all funds in the emergency kitty will be refunded to their respective owners.

8. Amendments & Legal Framework

Q: Can the welfare constitution be changed?
A: Yes. Amendments can only be made through a majority vote during an AGM or Special Meeting. Proposed amendments must be shared with members at least two (2) weeks prior to voting.
Q: What happens if there is a legal contradiction in the rules?
A: In the event of any oversight, contradiction, or deficiency in the welfare's constitution, the laws of the Republic of Kenya shall take absolute precedence.

APPENDIX: OFFICIALS' BYLAWS (FOR OFFICIAL USE)

The following bylaws provide operational guidelines for use by the MAJUSTWE Executive Committee and Officials.

1. Meeting Schedule

General meetings shall be held strictly on the first Saturday of every term.

2. Benefit & Contribution Tiers

When a bereavement occurs, mandatory contributions are strictly categorized as follows:

  • Loss of a Parent/Guardian: Ksh 200 per member.
  • Loss of a Principal Member / Spouse: Ksh 500 per member.

3. Claim Verification & Payout

All bereavement claims must first be verified by the respective Zonal Representatives and the Secretary before any processing can occur.

Once verified, the Treasurer is authorized to make an immediate 50% payout of the expected total to the bereaved member to assist with urgent logistics, prior to the collection of the remaining funds.

4. Discipline & Code of Conduct

  • Absences: Fines will be strictly levied against members who are absent from scheduled meetings (the exact penalty structure is determined by the executive, e.g., a Ksh 1,000 fine for 3 consecutive absences without a valid apology).
  • Conduct: Any member found in a state of inebriation during official welfare meetings or functions faces immediate expulsion from the welfare.
